Narration tracking prototype

Whole-paragraph Qwen generation, followed by windowed Whisper recognition. The moving highlight defaults to three words. Switch to two or five words, or a whole sentence, to compare.

Timing is approximate: these are the first accepted word timestamps, not timings corrected after listening to the entire recording. Smaller highlights make timing errors easier to notice.

Playback replays the recorded estimates. Details show whether recognition ran concurrently with generation. Word-match percentages measure text correspondence, not timestamp accuracy.
